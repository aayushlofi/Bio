General Instructions :

  1. This question paper consists of 39 questions in 5 sections (Section A, Section B, Section C, Section D and Section E).
  2. All questions are compulsory. However, an internal choice of approximately 33% is provided in some questions. A student is expected to attempt only one of these choices.
  3. Section A consists of 20 objective-type questions (Q. Nos. 1 to 20) carrying 1 mark each. Questions 1 to 16 are Multiple-Choice Questions (MCQs) and Questions 17 to 20 are Assertion–Reason based questions.
  4. Section B consists of 6 Very Short Answer (VSA) type questions (Q. Nos. 21 to 26) carrying 2 marks each. Answers to these questions should be in the range of 30 to 50 words.
  5. Section C consists of 7 Short Answer (SA) type questions (Q. Nos. 27 to 33) carrying 3 marks each. Answers to these questions should be in the range of 50 to 80 words.
  6. Section D consists of 3 Long Answer (LA) type questions (Q. Nos. 34 to 36) carrying 5 marks each. Answers to these questions should be in the range of 80 to 120 words.
  7. Section E consists of 3 Source-based / Case-based / Competency-based integrated units of assessment (Q. Nos. 37 to 39) carrying 4 marks each with sub-parts of the values of 1, 1 and 2 marks.
  8. Draw neat, proportionate and well-labelled biological diagrams wherever required.
